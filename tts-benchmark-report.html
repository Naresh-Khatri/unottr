What matters

  • TheRock detected the RX 6700 XT as gfx1031 without an architecture override.
  • Kokoro GPU was about 2.2 times faster than Kokoro CPU.
  • Kokoro GPU needs warm-up. Initial unseen input shapes took 11 to 37 seconds.
  • Piper returned a sentence chunk in roughly 38 to 96 milliseconds.
  • Chatterbox Turbo was too slow and used a 3.8 GiB model cache.
  • Stable Chatterbox 0.1.7 did not include the documented Nano model.
  • F5-TTS installed an NVIDIA-linked audio component. A SoundFile workaround was required.
  • Kokoro, Chatterbox, and F5 returned complete audio. Piper streamed by sentence.

Decision

Use a Piper-compatible voice through sherpa-onnx-node. Keep it warm in a dedicated utility process.

Ship no voice in the application. Download one approved public-domain-data voice during onboarding or from Settings.
